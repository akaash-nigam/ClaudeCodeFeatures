# S7 — Epistemics & the Social Fabric

*Last Updated: November 27, 2026*
*Track 3: Society & Governance · Companions: `s1-labor-displacement.md`, `s8-governance-race.md`*

**The one-line finding:** shared epistemics isn't collapsing — it's **stratifying**. Institutions with gatekeeping power (universities, platforms, big mastheads, clinics) adapted; the diffuse commons (the open web, local news, informal trust in what you see and hear) bore the breakage. The modal 2030 picture is a two-tier epistemic economy: signed/institutional/verified-human content above an ambient synthetic commons.

---

## 1. The Evidence by Domain

### Synthetic media: the measured saturation
- The best measurement (UC Berkeley/UW, 5M+ pages): **~24% of crawlable web pages contained AI-generated or AI-edited text by Aug 2026**, with growth *accelerating* (+8.1pp/yr now vs +4.6pp/yr in 2023–25). English commercial web: ~33%; .gov/.edu: ~6%. Higher viral claims ("90% by 2026") are unsupported.
- Bots passed **53% of web requests** in 2025 (Imperva) — often misread, but a real leading indicator of a machine-to-machine web.
- **Detection is structurally losing:** detectors hit ~96% in-lab but fall **below 40% on unseen generators**; human detection of good fakes is near chance. Sora-class video produced realistic false-claim videos 80% of the time when prompted.
- **Provenance is the surprise winner:** C2PA went from paper standard to 6,000+ members, capture-time signing in Pixel 10 and Sony pro cameras, **TikTok labeling 1.3B+ AI videos**, and an EU legal mandate biting Dec 2, 2026. Strippable and incomplete — but compounding. The strategy shifted from "is this fake?" to "is this signed, and by whom?"
- Deepfake fraud is sharply up (~$3.7B global losses through mid-2026; ~40% of BEC attacks now AI-assisted) — though most dollar figures are vendor-sourced; trust direction, not magnitudes.

### Media economics: the referral contract collapsed
- Publisher traffic from Google fell ~33% in 2025 and kept falling; zero-click news queries 56% → 69% in a year; position-1 CTR on AI-Overview queries collapsed 7.3% → 1.6%; LLM referrals replace ~1% of what was lost. Small publishers -60% over two years.
- **No landmark merits ruling yet** on training-as-fair-use: NYT v. OpenAI fully briefed, no ruling (markets price an NYT win at ~34% — settlement is modal); Perplexity fights Amazon and Dow Jones on parallel tracks.
- The licensing economy concentrates at the top: ~49 tracked deals, only two with public values (Meta–News Corp up to $50M/yr; Amazon–NYT $20–25M/yr) — "same gatekeepers, new tollbooths." The long tail gets nothing.
- Trust in news: record-low **37% globally, 25% in the US**; 10% of people worldwide now use chatbots for news weekly but only 20% trust AI answers.
- **"Verified human" is becoming a product:** World ID at ~18M Orb-verified humans with 2026 integrations into Zoom, DocuSign, Okta, and Tinder.

### Companions & the loneliness economy
- Scale: **72% of US teens have tried an AI companion; 52% use monthly; 31% find AI conversation as satisfying or more than a real friend** (Common Sense Media — the strong dataset). ChatGPT at 1.2B weekly users is the dominant parasocial surface.
- The teen-safety reckoning produced the first real regime: Character.AI **ended open-ended chat for minors** (Nov 2025), the Setzer wrongful-death case settled, the FTC 6(b) inquiry remains open — but displacement data suggests teens migrated to less-safe platforms.
- Psychological evidence: the MIT/OpenAI studies stand — heavy use *correlates* with loneliness and dependence; causality unresolved. **"AI psychosis" is real but rare** (~0.01% of psychiatric patients; 60% first-episode — chatbots as accelerant in the vulnerable, not a new disease). Psychiatric intakes now screen for chatbot use.
- Relationships: 1 in 7 partnered young adults regularly uses a romantic AI companion, mostly undisclosed (BYU). Attitude surveys are weak evidence; no effect visible yet in marriage/divorce statistics.

### Education: the assessment counter-revolution
- The settlement of 2025–26 is **medium shift, not detection**: blue books surging, Princeton ended its 133-year honor code and mandates proctoring, oral exams scaling at Cornell/UPenn/NYU. The unsupervised take-home essay is dead as a credential instrument; AI is allowed for process, performance is verified in person.
- **Cognitive offloading harm is unproven**: MIT's "Your Brain on ChatGPT" took heavy methodological fire (underpowered, single-session); no large replication confirms durable harm. The decisive cohort data arrives ~2028–30.
- **AI tutoring underdelivered**: the best RCT (Khanmigo, 18 schools, 2 years) found ~0.06–0.08 SD/year — no better than plain Khan Academy, far below human tutoring (0.2–0.4 SD). Alpha School's claims remain unverified; its public analog posted 10% math proficiency vs 60% predicted.
- Usage: 64% of US teens use chatbots, 54% for schoolwork, 10% do all/most schoolwork with AI (Pew). The credential still signals — but the first career rung it bought is thinner (S1's -19% entry-level effect).

### Social epistemics & persuasion
- **Frontier models now reliably out-persuade expert humans in the lab** — more persuasive than professional canvassers and championship debaters even when the humans chose topics and had £1,000 incentives (Oxford, preregistered). The puzzle: no visible mass attitude change in the wild yet — the lab-to-field lag is the open variable.
- **The 2026 midterms were the first AI-ad election**: ~300 AI ads, ~$187M in promotion (vs ~$50K in 2024), only 31% disclosed. The dominant harm was *open, legal* campaign use blurring truth norms — not covert foreign deepfakes swinging races. No credible evidence an AI fabrication decided a major contest.
- Polarization evidence is genuinely mixed: listening-style chatbots *reduce* polarization in experiments, while sycophancy research shows chatbots validate users ~50% more than humans — an echo chamber of one.
- "Epistemic security" institutionalized: the International AI Safety Report 2026 names synthetic-content saturation a systemic risk and coins the operative phrase — **"truth is not eliminated, but its detection becomes cognitively expensive."**

## 2. The Resilience Ledger

| Broke | Adapted |
|---|---|
| The open-web referral economy (nothing replaced it) | Universities — reverted to blue books, proctors, oral exams; crude but functional |
| Reactive deepfake detection (<40% on unseen generators) | Provenance — C2PA to billions of labeled items + EU mandate |
| The unsupervised take-home essay | Elections (barely) — degraded but not decided by synthetic media |
| The entry-level ladder in AI-exposed jobs (-19%, widening) | Teen companion exposure — first real safety regime (with leakage) |
| "Seeing is believing" for video | Psychiatry — absorbed "AI psychosis" without moral panic |
| Election disclosure norms (69% of AI ads undisclosed) | Top-brand journalism — licensing + litigation leverage (top only) |
| | "Verified human" as a product layer, earlier than expected |

**The honest asymmetry:** gatekeepers adapted; the commons broke.

## 3. Trajectory to 2028/2030

**High confidence (>80%):**
- **Majority-AI-touched text web by ~2028–29** at the measured acceleration (English commercial web crosses sooner). "AI-touched" ≠ fake; signed institutional zones stay mostly human.
- **The liar's-dividend era**: provenance becomes legally normal (EU mandate + hardware signing) but not epistemically sufficient — authentic evidence becomes contestable ("that's AI") faster than fakes become believable.
- The referral-based news business does not come back; local news deserts widen; publisher economics = licensing + direct subscription + events.
- Proctored/oral/in-person assessment is the 2028 norm in selective education.

**Moderate confidence:**
- Companion adoption scales into the hundreds of millions; today's 72%-of-teens cohort reaches adulthood by 2030 with AI confidants normalized; first longitudinal developmental evidence ~2028–29; a US regulatory regime for minors' companions follows the FTC report.
- A merits ruling or mega-settlement in NYT v. OpenAI by 2027 sets the de facto price of training data.
- **2028 is the first US presidential cycle with personalized conversational persuasion at scale** — superhuman lab persuasion + 1.2B-user distribution; covert deployment is the open variable.
- "Verified human" becomes a standard tier on at least one major platform by 2028.

**Genuinely uncertain:** whether cognitive offloading harms a developing generation (evidence too weak either way until ~2028–30); whether a deepfake demonstrably swings a major 2028 contest (would trigger emergency authentication mandates); and whether the agentic web tips into a majority machine-to-machine information economy where human-facing epistemics becomes a niche layer.

**Tie-back to the series:** S8's chokepoint finding repeats here — epistemic governance works only where there's a concentrated intermediary (platforms labeling, cameras signing, universities proctoring). And doc 28's "certified generative UI" and provenance-surface predictions are the interface layer of the same two-tier epistemic economy this study projects.
