# Society & Geopolitics Research Plan

*Created: November 27, 2026*
*Builds on: md/26 (chat apps), md/27 (interface landscape), md/28 (capabilities 2028–2030)*

Eight studies in three tracks. Each study = parallel research agents + one synthesis doc in this folder, ending with a 2028/2030 projection tied back to doc 28's capability table.

## Track 1: Economics & Labor

- **S1 — White-Collar Displacement** (`s1-labor-displacement.md`) — occupation-by-exposure matrix; automation vs augmentation evidence through 2026 (RLI, Cowork usage study, Anthropic Economic Index, entry-level hiring debate); 2028/2030 projection.
- **S2 — Token Economy & Concentration** (`s2-token-economy.md`) — who captures value across the stack (labs/infra/harness/apps); circular financing; bubble scenarios; agent-labor pricing.
- **S3 — The Human Premium** (`s3-human-premium.md`) — what appreciates: judgment, accountability, licensure, trust, in-person work, verification skill.

## Track 2: Geopolitics

- **S4 — The Compute War** (`s4-compute-war.md`) — US–China chips/export controls state 2026; Chinese model trajectory; Taiwan risk; sovereign AI; power map of the stack.
- **S5 — Energy Geopolitics** (`s5-energy-geopolitics.md`) — the gigawatt race; US vs China grid buildout; gas/nuclear/Gulf plays; datacenter diplomacy.
- **S6 — AI in Warfare & Statecraft** (`s6-warfare-statecraft.md`) — autonomous weapons, drone lessons, cyber offense/defense, intelligence automation, influence ops.

## Track 3: Society & Governance

- **S7 — Epistemics & Social Fabric** (`s7-epistemics-society.md`) — synthetic media, companions/loneliness, education rupture, cognitive offloading, media economics after answer engines.
- **S8 — The Governance Race** (`s8-governance-race.md`) — EU AI Act vs US reality; agent liability; international coordination; lab self-governance vs the 110-day doubling.

## Sequencing

Wave 1: S1 → S4 → S2 (strongest data; feed each other)
Wave 2: S5 + S8 ("can institutions keep up")
Wave 3: S7 + S6 + S3 (synthesis layer)
