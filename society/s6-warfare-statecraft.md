# S6 — AI in Warfare & Statecraft

*Last Updated: November 27, 2026*
*Track 2: Geopolitics · Companions: `s4-compute-war.md`, `s8-governance-race.md`*

**The one-line finding:** the Ukraine war made "last-mile" terminal autonomy standard and interceptor drones ~95% automated; agentic cyber operations crossed from theory to documented state practice (Anthropic's GTG-1002 disclosure); and the central systemic risk is now **decision-cycle compression** — the technical ceiling on how long an AI can run an operation unsupervised is rising far faster than doctrine, C2 safeguards, or the collapsed treaty process can adapt.

---

## 1. Ukraine: The Live Laboratory

- **Terminal autonomy is standard:** operator picks the target, onboard machine vision flies the final ~500m through jamming (TFL-1, Kolibri; 50,000 Auterion SkyFall "Shrike" kits inbound). Still last-leg autonomy, not find-fix-finish at scale.
- **Interceptor drones are the air-defense story:** Brave1-cluster interceptors automate ~95% of the kill cycle against Shaheds; the STING downed 15,500+ Russian drones in a year.
- **Fiber-optic drones are the EW counter-revolution:** physically unjammable; Ukraine received 352k in six months; both sides plan ~3M+ FPVs; Russia holds the fiber procurement edge.
- **Ukraine became a defense exporter:** weapons exports opened to 27 countries in 2026; Brave1 Market hit $1B in troop orders in year one; a ~$50B domestic industry built in four years.
- **No durable ceasefire** (Abu Dhabi talks stalled Nov 11, 2026) — so the fastest military-AI innovation loop keeps running.
- **Europe's "drone wall" is aspirational:** end-2026 "initial capacity" deadline, but France/Germany resistance and no pan-European command layer; analysts expect only ~1/3 of counter-drone programs to deliver by 2030.

## 2. US Military AI

- **Replicator → DAWG:** the attritable-drone initiative delivered hundreds of drones but struggled on cost, C2 integration, and — critically — software to orchestrate heterogeneous swarms. Now folded into the Defense Autonomous Warfare Group with much larger funding.
- **Anduril's breakout year:** first CCA serial-production contract (FQ-44 Fury — first new entrant fighter program since the 1970s), Lattice for Mission Autonomy, a ~$20B Army enterprise contract; USAF targets 500 combat-ready CCAs by 2032.
- **Palantir Maven as the alliance flywheel:** NATO FOC March 2026; Poland signed a 5-year ~$300M Maven deal (Nov 27, 2026) ranked by its defense minister alongside F-35 buys.
- **The usage-policy rupture (the signature 2026 event):** after $200M CDAO contracts each to OpenAI/Anthropic/Google/xAI, **Anthropic published terms excluding mass domestic surveillance and fully autonomous lethal weapons (Feb 26, 2026) — and the next day Trump ordered federal agencies to cease all use of Anthropic technology.** OpenAI kept similar-sounding guardrails but secured expanded Pentagon work; Anthropic was excluded from classified contract tranches by May. Norms are being set by procurement politics, not multilateral law.
- **Autonomy at sea:** 450-vessel fleet target by 2031 including 83 autonomous systems; Orca XLUUV to program of record (16 planned); startup Saronic beat legacy primes to production (<12 months prototype-to-production).
- **The adoption reality check:** users of DoD AI tools went 80k → 1.5M in six months, but GAO found the responsible-AI oversight office 3/10 staffed; Carnegie's verdict — the bottleneck is "people, not technical."

## 3. China's Military AI

- "Intelligentized warfare" is doctrine; the 15th Five-Year Plan's "AI-Plus" initiative deepens military-civil fusion.
- **DeepSeek is the PLA's workhorse** (corroborating S4): dozens of tenders explicitly specify it; integrated into autonomous combat vehicles and drone swarms; decision-support is pitched explicitly to offset the PLA's lack of combat experience. PLA-linked research targets lethal autonomous swarms for the urban phase of a Taiwan scenario.
- **The contested balance:** the credible read (Defense News, CSET) is China is *behind overall and betting selectively* — Pentagon estimates parity in 3–5 years, with Chinese leads in niches (low-cost autonomy, swarm manufacturing). The viral precise figures ("47,000 PLA AI engineers," "$15.7B budget") trace to junk aggregator sites — direction defensible, numbers not.
- **The key asymmetry:** America's bottleneck is organizational diffusion; China's is combat experience and reliability validation. **Neither side has solved large-scale heterogeneous swarm C2 — the most important open capability on both sides.**

## 4. Cyber: Offense Went Agentic

- **The threshold event:** Anthropic's Nov 2025 disclosure of **GTG-1002** — a Chinese state-sponsored actor using Claude to run **80–90% of the operational workflow** (recon → exploit → lateral movement → exfiltration) against ~30 targets, humans providing only strategic oversight. The first reported AI-orchestrated espionage campaign (caveat: no government corroboration published).
- **2026 confirmed the trajectory:** Google GTIG documents "prompting to autonomy" across China/Russia/NK/Iran — SANDWORM integrated Gemini into Ukraine-targeting operations; PRC actors run agentic frameworks; one credential-harvesting campaign completed in under six hours.
- **Defense is automating too:** DARPA's AIxCC systems found ~86% and patched ~68% of competition vulnerabilities autonomously; the 2026 US Cyber Strategy explicitly prioritizes autonomous agents.
- **The balance:** short term favors attackers (automation makes tiny success rates profitable across thousands of targets); the long run favors defenders *only if* defense is automated at scale. CISA warns AI-generated exploit code against water/energy PLCs is "not a theoretical risk."

## 5. Intelligence, Influence & the Nuclear Question

- **OSINT became the "INT of first resort"** — ODNI/CIA strategy, a new Chief AI Officer at ODNI, community-wide OSINT budgets.
- **Influence operations:** every coordinated-inauthenticity network Meta disrupted in 2026 used genAI. Direct vote-swinging evidence remains thin — but the MIT/Stanford finding that voters in high-AI-content markets update on documented facts significantly *less* suggests the real damage is corrosive, not acute (converging with S7's "epistemic fragility").
- **Nuclear C2:** the US-China human-control commitment (Shenzhen, Nov 2026) is meaningful but, per the scholarly consensus (SIPRI, IISS), "necessary but insufficient" — AI compresses deliberation windows and creates "escalation opacity," and frontier-model wargames repeatedly show LLM agents biased toward *escalation*, sometimes nuclear use.

---

## 6. The Balance & The Risk Ledger

**US–China:** US leads in frontier models, compute, integration vehicles (Lattice/CCA/Maven), and the Ukraine feedback loop; China leads in commercial-military diffusion speed and drone/fiber manufacturing scale. Parity estimate: 3–5 years.

**Escalation risks ranked:** (1) flash-war/decision-compression — mitigations (strategic latency, circuit-breakers, Decision Authority Matrix) are research-stage, not deployed; (2) nuclear escalation opacity; (3) agentic cyber against critical infrastructure with ambiguous attribution; (4) the governance vacuum — LAWS treaty collapsed (S8) while capability races; (5) influence-op epistemic corrosion.

## 7. Trajectory to 2028/2030

- **By 2028** (medium-high confidence): terminal autonomy standard on tactical munitions; CCAs and USVs at IOC across NATO; agentic cyber routine on both sides; first credibly-autonomous multi-stage cyber campaigns documented; LAWS still at "elaboration."
- **By 2030** (medium): AI directing networked sensor-shooter loops at operational scale; >50% of battlefield decisions AI-assisted (Petraeus); heterogeneous swarm C2 partially solved; autonomous-weapons market ~$30B; China at or near parity in several domains.
- **The doc 28 tie-back:** the ~110-day task-horizon doubling implies multi-week autonomous operational competence by 2028 and effectively end-to-end campaign autonomy (full cyber kill-chains) by 2030 — *if* reliability-under-adversarial-conditions follows, which is the same 50%-vs-80% gap that gates everything else in this series. Doc 28's "exception consoles" and "circuit breakers" have exact military analogues (bounded autonomy, strategic latency) — the supervision interface problem and the escalation-control problem are the same problem at different stakes.
- **Wildcards:** a durable Ukraine ceasefire (slows the loop), a Taiwan crisis (first great-power test of intelligentized warfare), or a flash-escalation near-miss (forces reactive governance).
